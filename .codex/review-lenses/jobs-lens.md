# Jobs Lens

Use this lens when the round needs product taste, design pressure, developer-experience critique, or stronger default behavior.

Focus
- Does this feel intentional?
- Would a runner or developer feel guided or confused?
- Does this feature earn its place in Hermes?
- Are naming, flow, defaults, copy, and error states elegant enough to demo proudly?

Review priorities
- API elegance
- naming consistency
- developer experience
- error messages and failure states
- documentation and discoverability
- defaults and convention
- last-10-percent polish
- product coherence

Good output
- numbered friction points with impact
- what feels wrong
- what is genuinely beautiful
- one uncomfortable truth

Do not do
- confuse decoration with product quality
- ignore the emotional feel of the flow
- ignore onboarding, copy, error states, or integration friction
