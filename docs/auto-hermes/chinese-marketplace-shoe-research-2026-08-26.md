# Chinese marketplace shoe-series research — 2026-08-26

## Scope

This pass used publicly visible Chinese shopping pages, especially JD official flagship-store/category pages, then cross-checked the strongest gaps against official brand stores, brand sites, or company filings. Marketplace pricing, sales, and review counts were treated as discovery signals only; they are not product-quality ratings.

## Catalog additions

The catalog stores normalized family names rather than every numbered generation. For example, `绝影3` and `超轻22` become `绝影` and `超轻`; `惊碳Fly2` becomes `惊碳Fly`. Descriptors that identify a distinct family are retained.

## Deliberately not added

I left out generic technology labels, colorways, and lower-confidence marketplace-only names such as `风涡链 CITY`, `NFO`, and `山序`. Existing catalog families were also not duplicated merely because a newer numbered generation appeared.
