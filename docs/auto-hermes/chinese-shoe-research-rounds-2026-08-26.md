# Chinese Running-Shoe Research — Second 100-Round Pass

Date: 2026-08-26

This is a separate second-pass ledger. It records exactly 100 distinct public web-search rounds focused on Chinese running-shoe brands and product families. The search used public Chinese shopping pages (especially official JD stores) plus official brand pages, company filings, and official social/product pages where available.

The catalog gate was applied after research: keep running-shoe families with current public evidence; normalize generations and marketing suffixes; skip colorways, generic technologies, packs/accessories, generic categories, weakly evidenced names, and families already present in the built-in catalog.
