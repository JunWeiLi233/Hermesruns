# Chinese Running-Shoe Research — Third 100-Round Pass

Date: 2026-08-26

This is a separate continuation ledger. It records exactly 100 distinct public web-search rounds focused on Chinese running-shoe brands and product families. The search used public Chinese shopping pages (especially official or brand-operated JD pages) plus official brand sites, company filings, and verified brand social/product pages where available.

The catalog gate was applied after research: keep distinct running-shoe families with current public evidence; normalize generations and marketing suffixes; skip colorways, generic technologies, packs/accessories, generic categories, weakly evidenced names, and families already present in the built-in catalog.

- ANTA `Saker`, `Wind Wander`, and `Swift` were retained as research leads only because this catalog update is scoped to the Chinese-market family set.
