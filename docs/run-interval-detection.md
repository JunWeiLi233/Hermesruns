# Automatic run intervals

Session analysis estimates intervals on read when a run has neither saved segment declarations nor explicit imported roles. Generic, unlabelled auto-laps do not prevent detection. Source laps, telemetry, activity totals and declarations remain unchanged. An empty saved declaration also takes precedence.

The detector groups recorded cumulative-distance samples into approximately 20-second elapsed windows. Gaps over 10 seconds are excluded. Distance resets, conflicting timestamps and implausible jumps disable detection. Moving windows establish a within-run baseline (35th percentile) and median. The faster entry threshold needs at least 18% contrast, 0.35 m/s absolute contrast, and 10% above the moving median. A lower exit threshold limits boundary chatter. Efforts last at least 60 seconds; an isolated effort needs 120 seconds. At least 40 seconds must cross the faster entry threshold, or at least 20 seconds plus a measured HR rise of 5 bpm relative to the preceding 120 seconds. Short, steady and incomplete runs can have no detected work.

Two supported efforts separated by at most 120 seconds stay together if the intervening moving speed is at least 90% of the slower flanking effort. A covered HR decline of at least 5 bpm can retain a recovery during a shallow dip lasting at least 40 seconds. Clear pace slowdowns remain recovery even with elevated HR. Unknown or stationary windows prevent merging. Earlier context may extend an effort only if that context has its own covered HR rise; it must start within 180 seconds of the faster entry window. Unseeded pace undulations cannot chain through the easy prelude or tail. These numeric cutoffs are reproducible detector heuristics, not physiological standards.

Moving sections before and after efforts are estimated warm-up and cool-down; edge fragments under 60 seconds stay unknown. Moving sections between efforts are estimated recovery. Stationary windows and gaps remain unknown. This identifies relative pace changes, including faster finishes; it cannot establish workout intent, threshold intensity or physiological zones. HR remains separately integrated. HR-assisted decisions require at least 80% coverage in each compared window; missing or sparse HR is not invented or used as evidence. Clear pace efforts and recoveries can be detected without HR.

The API marks estimates `auto-detected` and measurements `derived-stream`. Every boundary comes from observed samples. IDs encode elapsed boundaries and stay stable across reloads. The editor can turn reviewed estimates into runner declarations using the existing save contract.

Work-only comparison initially selects detected work. Manual deselection, including deselecting every segment, persists during refresh. Imported and runner-declared work retain the existing explicit-selection behavior. Session selection and condition-matching rules remain explicit.

Validation includes dense repeated efforts, sustained faster finishes, steady runs, stops, short runs, isolated spikes, sparse data, distance resets, missing HR, authoritative source labels, saved declarations, measured boundaries and comparison deselection. Local real-run QA uses read-only telemetry and the live signed-in browser; no declarations are written automatically.
